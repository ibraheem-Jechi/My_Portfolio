About

The person
behind the lens

I'm Alex Reeves — a creative director and visual storyteller based in Los Angeles. For over a decade I've shaped identities for cultural institutions, luxury brands, and independent filmmakers who believe that beauty is a form of intelligence.

My process begins in the margins: with half-written sentences, with the quality of afternoon light, with what is left deliberately unsaid. I translate these instincts into work that resonates long after first contact.
